Insight & coaching

An AI coach that has read your data

Every morning UpVital writes one personalized insight from your own numbers. Have a question? The coach answers with your trends in hand - not generic advice.

Today's insight

Your recovery is up 11% this week

Three nights of 7h+ sleep lifted your overnight HRV. You're primed for a harder session today - just keep an eye on that afternoon caffeine, which has been landing after 4pm.

Why is my sleep score lower than last week?
You're sleeping about the same total time, but deep sleep dropped ~18 min a night and you woke more often after 3am. Later screen time or that evening espresso are the usual culprits.

Your data

It stays yours

Apple Health powers everything - and it's entirely under your control.

Opt-in by design

Connecting Apple Health is optional and prompted only when you choose. Nothing syncs until you say so.

Encrypted & per-user

Data travels over HTTPS and is stored with per-user access controls. Your account only ever sees your own data.

Leave anytime

Disconnect Apple Health in device settings, or delete your account and data from the app in one tap.

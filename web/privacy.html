Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 August 2026 · Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what data UpVital ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. UpVital is operated by Shkelqim Surkishi, an individual based in Kosovo, who is the data controller responsible for your personal data. Please read it to understand how we handle your data.

Information we collect

• Account details: your email address, and a display name if you add one.
• Health and fitness data you choose to connect from Apple Health - sleep (including sleep stages), heart rate, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, blood oxygen, body temperature, steps, active energy, workouts, body weight, body fat, and date of birth - used to compute your daily scores.
• Profile inputs you enter: birthdate (for biological-age estimates), height, goals (steps, sleep, active energy, and a weight goal with a target date), and your notification and AI settings.
• Approximate location, only when you use the air-quality feature, to look up local air quality. It is used for that request and not stored as a location history.
• Caffeine entries you log.
• The messages you send to the AI coach, if you turn on AI insights & Coach (used to answer you; we don't store the conversation).
• Your device's time zone, so your daily scores follow your local calendar day.
• A push-notification token for your device, if you allow notifications.
• If you write to us through the contact form on upvital.app: your name, email address, message and browser type, used to reply to you.

An email address is needed to create an account. Everything else is optional: without Apple Health there are no scores, without location no air quality, and without AI insights & Coach no daily insight or coach chat.

How we use your information

We use your data to calculate your Recovery, Sleep, Stress, and Daily scores, to show local air quality, to send the notifications you enable and, if you turn on AI insights & Coach, to write your daily insight and coach replies. Scores are calculated automatically from your data; we make no automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use third-party tracking or analytics.

Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

UpVital is offered to people in the EU, so the GDPR applies to how we handle your data, even though we are based outside the EU. We rely on these legal bases:
• Your account, syncing, scores, export and deletion: to provide the app you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Health data from Apple Health: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)), given when you tap Connect.
• Daily insights and coach replies written by our AI provider: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)), given when you turn on AI insights & Coach.
• Air quality from your approximate location: your consent through the iOS location permission (Art. 6(1)(a)); turn it off anytime in iOS Settings.
• Push notifications: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); turn them off in Profile or iOS Settings.
• Account and security emails: to provide the app (Art. 6(1)(b)).
• Contact form: our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f)).
• Bot checks, rate limits and server logs: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object to processing based on legitimate interests by emailing us.
You can withdraw consent at any time: disconnect Apple Health, switch off AI insights & Coach in Profile, or delete your account. Withdrawing doesn't affect processing that happened before.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app, each bound by a data processing agreement and given only what it needs:
• Our database and sign-in provider - sign-in and storage of your account and health data, in the EU (Ireland).
• Our AI provider (USA) - only if you turn on AI insights & Coach. It writes your daily insight and the coach's replies. For the daily insight we send that day's Sleep and Recovery scores, the previous day's Stress and Daily scores, and your biological-age estimate. For the coach we send your message and the recent conversation, plus your age, step and sleep goals, your last 7 days of scores, that day's steps, active energy, resting heart rate, HRV and sleep (including deep and REM sleep), and your workouts from the last 7 days (type, duration, energy and average heart rate). Our AI provider does not use this to train its models and deletes it within 30 days, unless it must keep it longer to enforce its usage policy or by law.
• An air-quality data service - receives an approximate location to return air-quality readings; no account identifier is sent.
• Our server host - runs the server that the app talks to; your requests pass through it.
• Our website and security network - hosts this website and sits in front of our server for security and delivery; its bot check helps keep bots out of sign-up, log-in and the contact form.
• Our email provider - delivers the emails we send you (sign-up confirmation, welcome, security notices such as a changed password, and account closure) and forwards contact-form messages to our support inbox. It sends from the EU (Ireland).
• Apple - delivers push notifications to your device, if you allow them. The daily-insight notification includes the start of your insight.
You can ask us for the names of these providers at any time.

International data transfers

Your account and health data are stored in the EU (Ireland), and our emails are sent from the EU (Ireland). UpVital is run from Kosovo, which has no EU adequacy decision; the GDPR still applies in full to how we handle your data. Some providers may process data outside the EU/EEA: our AI provider (USA, only with AI insights & Coach on), Apple (USA, push notifications), our website and security network (global) and our server host (which may run the server in the USA). For each, the transfer is covered by the provider's EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification where it has one, or otherwise by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in its data processing agreement. You can ask us for details of these safeguards.

Data retention and your controls

How long we keep data: your account, profile, health data, scores and daily insights are kept until you delete them (Delete my data) or your account (Delete my account). Coach conversations aren't stored by us; our AI provider deletes what it receives within 30 days, unless it must keep it longer to enforce its usage policy or by law. Push tokens are kept until you log out, delete your account, or the token stops working. Contact-form messages are deleted automatically 12 months after they arrive, or sooner on request. Deleted data can stay in encrypted database backups for a short time, until those backups are replaced. Server logs, which can include IP addresses, are kept by our server host for a short time for security and troubleshooting, and our sign-in provider keeps sign-in records, including IP addresses, for a limited time for security.

You can export all of your data at any time from Profile → Data & privacy → "Export my data". "Delete my data" removes your health samples, workouts, scores and insights but keeps your account; "Delete my account" permanently deletes your account and all associated data - your profile, health samples, workouts, metrics and insights. If you signed in with Apple, it also removes UpVital from your Apple ID.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the EU/EEA you have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can exercise the main rights directly in the app (export, delete, and switching AI insights & Coach off) or by contacting us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection supervisory authority, or with the Information and Privacy Agency of Kosovo (AIP).

Our website

upvital.app does not use analytics or advertising and sets no cookies of its own. Our website host may set a strictly necessary security cookie, and its bot check runs on the contact form. The site's fonts are served from upvital.app itself, so your browser doesn't contact any outside font service.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in our EU database with per-user access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children

UpVital is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect, and update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about your privacy, or want to exercise your rights? Email support@upvital.app.
